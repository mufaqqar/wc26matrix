Scene 2 — The Azteca Fortress
Scene 02 Why the Azteca is the most intimidating stadium in world football
Voiceover

The Estadio Azteca is not just a stadium — it is a weapon. Opened in 1966, it has hosted two World Cup finals: 1970 and 1986. The altitude of 2,200 meters above sea level makes breathing difficult for visiting teams. The crowd of 87,000 is louder than any in football because of the stadium's unique architecture — the sound is trapped and amplified. Mexico has played dozens of World Cup matches here. Before England, no visiting team had ever won a knockout match at the Azteca. It was considered impossible. England proved it was not.
