Scene 3 — Why Knockout Goals Are Harder
Scene 03 The knockout stage is a different sport entirely
Voiceover

Knockout goals are statistically rarer than group stage goals for a simple reason: the stakes change everything. Teams play more conservatively. Defenses tighten. Nobody wants to make the mistake that ends their tournament. The average number of goals per game drops significantly in the knockout rounds. In the group stage, teams take risks. In the knockout stage, one error ends your World Cup. That is why so many legendary players have surprisingly few knockout goals. This is not a Ronaldo problem — it is a knockout football problem.
