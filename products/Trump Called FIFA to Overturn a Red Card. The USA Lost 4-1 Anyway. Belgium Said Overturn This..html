SCENE 08 Red card rules explained — what automatic suspension means

That's the rule. That's what Balogun was facing — missing the Belgium match. Appeals are possible but almost never successful. FIFA's disciplinary committee has the power to review and reduce or cancel suspensions. But in practice, red card reversals almost never happen. The Trump call made it happen. The question is: did the rules allow it, or did the phone call make the difference?
