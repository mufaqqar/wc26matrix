SCENE 02 What the injury actually is — tibial bone bruise and microfracture explained
Voiceover
Let's start with what Pulisic was actually playing through. A tibial bone bruise is exactly what it sounds like — a bruise on the bone itself, specifically the tibia, the main weight-bearing bone in your lower leg. A microfracture is a tiny crack in the bone. Not a full break that would snap your leg, but a stress fracture. Every step, every sprint, every kick — pain. The kind of pain that makes you subconsciously protect your leg. The kind of pain that slows you down even when your mind says push harder.
