Scene 2 — The Base Formation: What Brazil Actually Plays
Scene 02 4-3-3 with a twist — the false fullback system
Voiceover

On paper, Brazil lines up in a 4-3-3. Four defenders, three midfielders, three forwards. Standard enough. But watch closely — one fullback inverts into midfield when Brazil has the ball. This creates a box midfield, overloads the center, and leaves the wing exposed. It is a high-risk, high-reward system that works brilliantly — until it doesn't.

Scene 4 — The Midfield Problem: Creative Chaos
Scene 04 Three midfielders, two different jobs — the balance is off
Voiceover

Brazil's midfield three is the heart of the problem. They play with one defensive midfielder and two box-to-box creators. The issue? Both creators push forward, leaving the single pivot isolated against counter-attacks. Against Norway, Haaland exploited that space between the lines. One pass through the midfield and the entire defensive structure was compromised.
