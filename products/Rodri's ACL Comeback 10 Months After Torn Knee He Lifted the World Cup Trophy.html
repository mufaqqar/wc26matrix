SCENE 02 What is an ACL rupture? The injury explained
Voiceover
The ACL is a band of tissue inside your knee that connects your thigh bone to your shin bone. When it tears, your knee becomes unstable. Every pivot, every cut, every sprint feels like your leg might collapse. For an athlete who relies on turning and passing, it's the worst possible injury.
