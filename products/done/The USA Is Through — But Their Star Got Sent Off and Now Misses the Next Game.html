Scene 3 — How Red Card Suspensions Work
Scene 03 In the knockout stage, a red card means you miss the next match. No exceptions.
Voiceover

FIFA rules are clear. A straight red card in the knockout stage carries an automatic one-match suspension. There is no appeal process for tactical fouls. There is no discretion. The player is out. Period. This means that Balogun — the USA's top scorer, the man who just won them the match — will be sitting in the stands when the USA faces Belgium. He cannot be replaced on the roster. The USA must play the biggest knockout match of the tournament without their most dangerous attacker. That is the price of one mistimed tackle.
