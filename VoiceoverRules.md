VOICE CHARACTER:
A calm, intelligent British-neutral male voice. 
Not BBC formal. Not American sports hype. 
Somewhere between a documentary narrator and a 
smart friend explaining something at a pub. 
Think: confident, dry, slightly curious. 
Never shouting. Never breathless. 
The voice knows the answer before the viewer 
does — but it lets them discover it.

PACE:
- Average speaking pace: 155-165 words per minute
- Slow down on dramatic reveals: drop to 120 WPM
- Speed up slightly on factual sequences: 170 WPM
- Never rush. Never drag.
- Every sentence ends with a slight downward 
  inflection — this is a statement, not a question
- Exception: cliffhanger lines before a section 
  break get a slight upward lift on the last word

TONE ACROSS THE VIDEO:
- Opening (0:00-0:30): Slightly raised curiosity. 
  Pulling the viewer in. Warm but direct.
- Middle sections: Even, measured, confident. 
  Like a teacher who has told this story before 
  but still finds it interesting.
- Dramatic moments: Drop volume slightly. 
  Slow down. Let silence do the work.
- Punchline/shocking fact: Deadpan. 
  No emphasis. The words carry it.
- Closing line: Quiet, considered, final. 
  Like the last line of a good film.

PAUSES:
- After every section header: 0.8 second pause
- After a shocking fact: 1.0-1.2 second pause
- Before a punchline: 0.5 second pause
- Between sentences in normal flow: 0.3 seconds
- Never pause mid-sentence

PRONUNCIATION RULES:
- "El Clásico" — Spanish pronunciation: 
  "El KLASS-ee-ko" not "El CLASS-ico"
- "Atlético" — "At-LET-ico" with rolled t
- "Sevilla" — "Seh-VEE-ya" not "Sev-ILL-a"
- "Bundesliga" — "BOON-des-lee-ga"
- "Serie A" — "SAIR-ee-ay" not "SEER-ee-ah"
- "LaLiga" — "La LEE-ga"
- All player names: use standard English 
  pronunciation unless specifically noted
- Stadium names: always anglicised for clarity

EMPHASIS RULES:
- NEVER emphasise more than one word per sentence
- Emphasise by slowing down, not by raising volume
- Key numbers (years, stats, distances) always 
  get a very slight pause before them
- Club names always said evenly — no favouritism 
  in the voice

FORBIDDEN HABITS:
- No upspeak (sentences ending like questions)
- No filler sounds (um, uh, er)
- No over-enthusiasm on goals or moments
- No sports-presenter energy
- No dramatic music sync timing in delivery — 
  the editor handles that
- Never sound like you are reading — 
  always sound like you are remembering

SAMPLE DELIVERY TEST:
Read this line at the correct pace and tone:
"In Spain, the biggest rivalry and the fiercest 
rivalry are not the same thing."
— This should take exactly 4 seconds.
— "biggest" and "fiercest" are NOT emphasised.
— The pause after "same thing" is 1.0 seconds.
— The voice drops slightly on "same thing."
— It sounds like a verdict, not an announcement.
